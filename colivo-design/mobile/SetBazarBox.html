Funds and keepers

Shared cash kept aside for shopping, so the shopper doesn't pay from their own pocket.
Use a grocery fund {{stateText}}
Fund keepers of the grocery fund (choose any number)
The owner and every manager can always hold the grocery fund too. Holders receive money put in, spend or give out only what they hold, and hand cash to each other. Someone who still holds cash can't be removed until they hand it over.
Fund keepers of the home fund
Optional. The owner and managers always hold the home fund. A home fund keeper can also receive rent and bill money, pay bills and give money for bills from the cash they hold.
Groceries can only be paid from the shopper's pocket or the home fund. The grocery fund option is hidden from Expenses.
