Grocery fund

Shared cash kept aside for shopping, so the shopper doesn't pay from their own pocket.
Use a grocery fund {{stateText}}
This person gets the Fund keeper role. Only they can mark groceries as "paid from the grocery fund"; anyone can put money in. The owner or a manager can change the keeper. If no one is chosen, the manager keeps the grocery fund, or the owner if there's no manager.
Groceries can only be paid from the shopper's pocket or the home fund. The grocery fund option is hidden from Expenses.
